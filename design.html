How Foundations works

Foundations is a framework for a group of dynamic components that ensure efficiency-boosting and reflect our way of working.

To improve that, we made the Foundations segment for nurturing, collaborating, and unifying all the components of the system in our amazing brand.

About Mustache

Mustache is a functional Design System that centralizes and organizes every component in the app, allowing every team to find them easily and create a high-quality digital experience.
