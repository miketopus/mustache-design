This is Mustache. Our library to build powerful, amazing and uncommon digital experiences.

Since 2019 we've been building one of the most complete design systems at Rappi, shaped around the way our teams actually work.

It unifies our B2C and B2B products under one language, so every designer and engineer can connect and grow in an organized way.

Our purpose is simple: build consistent products at Rappi, and make sure our brand stays recognizable wherever it shows up, on every platform and format.

A polished UI builds trust and reduces friction. That belief is the reason Mustache exists.
